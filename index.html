Our Heritage & Craft

The Story of PaperNest

Founded with a passion for tactile craftsmanship, PaperNest bridges timeless paper tradition with contemporary minimalist design.

Crafting Joy in Every Stroke

In a world driven by pixels and screens, we believe in the physical magic of ink meeting fine paper. Every journal in our collection is crafted with acid-free, fountain-pen-friendly paper ranging from 120 GSM to 300 GSM watercolor paper.

We partner directly with small-batch paper mills and brass artisans to bring you stationery that is built to last a lifetime.

Eco-Conscious

Recycled cotton rag paper and plastic-free minimal packaging.

Zero Bleed-Through

Rigorously tested paper with fountain inks, acrylics, and gel pens.

Artisan Support

Supporting traditional Indian paper artisans and binderies.
